# Mixer follows the timeline

## What will change
- Open the mixer with one channel strip for every track currently shown in the timeline, followed by the Master strip.
- Keep bus, VCA, and aux strips hidden until their matching controls are selected.
- Remove the one-channel/all-channels switch because timeline channels will always stay synchronized with the mixer.
- Keep existing mixer controls, routing, meters, colors, and optional bus/VCA/aux controls intact.

## Verification
- Confirm adding or removing a timeline track immediately changes the mixer channel count.
- Confirm Master remains visible and buses, VCAs, and aux returns remain hidden until selected.
- Check the current build status and the mixer at desktop and narrow widths.

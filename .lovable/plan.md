# Mobile accessibility, gestures, landscape, and onboarding

## Accessibility settings
- Add a Mobile Accessibility section in Settings with independent switches for extra-large controls, increased text size, and high-contrast mode.
- Save these choices on the device and apply them immediately to phone navigation, tool panels, forms, dialogs, and track controls.
- Keep every option reversible and preserve the existing desktop and tablet styling.

## Phone track gestures
- Add touch gestures to each timeline track header: tap selects, long-press opens a large action menu, horizontal swipe toggles Mute or Solo, and vertical swipe moves the track one position.
- Show clear visual feedback and a short confirmation after each gesture.
- Keep visible action buttons in the long-press menu so gestures are never the only way to perform an action.

## Landscape editing
- Add a dedicated phone-landscape workspace with a compact transport and bottom navigation.
- Give the timeline more vertical space for arranging, widen beat controls into denser columns, and make the mixer horizontally scrollable with useful channel widths.
- Keep safe-area spacing and prevent page-level horizontal overflow.

## Mobile onboarding
- Show a short, dismissible first-run tour over the phone studio.
- Explain the larger controls and the five-button navigation: Home, Create, Record, Mix, and More.
- Include Back, Next, Skip, and Done controls plus a Settings action to replay the tour.

## Verification
- Test portrait and landscape phone sizes with a signed-in project.
- Verify all three accessibility modes persist after reload.
- Verify tap, long-press actions, swipe mute/solo, and swipe reorder on real timeline tracks.
- Verify the tour, five-button navigation, beat maker, timeline, and mixer remain usable with no clipping or runtime errors.
